# PINtPRESS editorial pass — 2 November 2026

Scope: 56 top-level article, guide, reference and series-hub files in `blog/`, plus blog shelf/archive copy. Drafts and generated stadium venue pages are outside this pass. Work is based on commit `ea8e12e2637cdceb2f5a5ff6dfc42995bc7f9c98`.

This is a targeted editorial pass, not a claim that every remaining sentence is free of formulaic writing or that every historical, financial or availability claim has been independently verified. Candidate passages were located across the corpus, then edited in context. The long reference tables, credited quotations and deliberately comic tournament rulings were not flattened into one house voice.

## What changed

- Cut paragraphs that announce why the preceding detail matters, repeated theses and multiple conclusions.
- Replaced invented contrasts and promotional verdicts with the observations already in the article.
- Removed motive-reading around tap selections, imports, brand strength and corporate decisions.
- Cut the accumulated speculative sections of the Whippet piece while retaining the beer boards, glassware, opening information and Cornish pictures.
- Removed the unsupported fortnight shelf-life claim from the Oktoberfest essay and trilogy hub. The brewing window for fresh hops is distinct from the finished beer’s availability.
- Corrected cask copy that said it could not be exported, while retaining the importance of handling and cellar work.
- Narrowed the Bell’s prose to changes in rateable value and the index’s categories; neither establishes its final bill or finances.
- Rewrote 18 article summaries and updated shelf, archive inputs and generated archive pages. Matched edited FAQ answers to their JSON-LD and regenerated llms-full.txt.

Ordinary comparisons, useful technical distinctions, jokes and title-separator punctuation are not prohibited by this pass.

## Verification and limitations

- All 232 JSON-LD blocks across 131 HTML files parse.
- Promotion-guard comparison finds no new failures in edited HTML. Three edited pages already lack JSON-LD: Beerology, Pick the Right Pint and September Is Oktoberfest. Those pre-existing omissions are not repaired by an editorial copy pass.
- Source, venue and companion links, analytics and image references are checked through the existing promotion guard. External sources were not re-fetched as a full factual audit.
- `git diff --check` passes.
- No publication dates, slugs, designs, datasets or draft articles have been changed. Nothing is merged or published by this pass.

## Source questions still requiring their own checks

1. Holiday beer: the claimed duty difference and repeated assertion of a 4.6% tax band need primary-source verification before they are relied on. A prose edit should not conceal that question.
2. Revivals: the alcohol-duty paragraph describes the 2023 change too broadly; the hop-category list also groups varieties with different origins and names under “American C-hops”. These need source-led corrections, not a stylistic substitute.
3. London: the account of ordinary beer failing the voyage and IPA being engineered for it needs checking against brewing history. The broad “every IPA” lineage sentence should not be treated as established by this copy pass.
4. Beerology: variety terminology and claims about sensory frameworks need a separate accuracy review; model-generated profiles must remain explicitly identified.
5. Older guides: dated tap sightings, counts, event details and screen-release dates have not been certified as current.
6. Obituaries and tournament pages: underlying biographical and sporting claims were not re-reported during the prose pass.
