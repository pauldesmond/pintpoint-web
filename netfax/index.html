Netfax

The news, as it looked in 1985. For iPhone and iPad.

Netfax turns today’s headlines into a 40×24, eight-colour teletext screen. Type a three-digit page, press the coloured keys, and let the headlines roll past on a 25-second carousel.

  • Bring your own news: add any RSS or Atom feed, or tap Add News for a starter pack. Every story credits its publisher and links to the original.
  • The web through 1980s eyes: type an address and Netfax redraws the page as teletext.
  • Weather on 400 from Apple Weather, money on 240 with ECB reference rates.
  • A simulated 1980s market screen on 690 — fictional, just for fun.
  • Quizzes with REVEAL, Teletext Camera, and a full-screen tour with original muzak.

No account, no tracking and no ads. Your feeds stay on your device.
