Netfax Support

Help for the Netfax teletext news reader on iPhone and iPad

Need help, found a bug or have a feed that won't load? Email hello@pintpoint.co.uk and include your device, iOS version and, if relevant, the feed address.

How do I add a feed?

On the home screen, tap Paste a feed. You can also open Feed Settings, choose a category and tap Paste a feed URL. Enter the address of a public RSS or Atom feed, give it a name and save. Its headlines appear on the category's teletext pages.

What does Add News do?

Add News adds the public RSS feeds that BBC News and Sky News publish to your device, for your personal reading. It downloads a small feed list from pintpoint.co.uk and sets up the news sections. Headlines and the publisher's summaries are shown with a credit line, and you can open the original article on the publisher's site. You can remove any feed in Feed Settings, or import a feed list from the Files app instead.

Why won't a feed load?

  • The address must use HTTPS. Plain http:// feeds are refused.
  • It must be on a public website. Local network addresses, IP addresses, custom ports and addresses with a username or password are blocked for safety.
  • It must be an actual RSS or Atom feed, not a normal web page. Try the site's /feed or /rss link.
  • Very large feeds and sites that block apps may fail. Netfax keeps the last good copy and tries again later.

Weather

Page 400 shows a forecast from Apple Weather for the UK place you choose. Netfax does not use your location. If no forecast appears, check your internet connection and try again later.

Which page numbers should I know?

  • 100: index of all sections
  • 101: news headlines
  • 300: sport
  • 400: weather
  • 240: exchange rates (ECB reference rates)

Type any three-digit page on the remote, or use the coloured buttons.

The page keeps changing. How do I stop it?

Long stories rotate through sub-pages (1/3, 2/3...) every 25 seconds. Tap HOLD to freeze the current sub-page, and tap it again to carry on from where it was.

Privacy

Netfax has no account and collects no data. Read the Netfax privacy policy.
