How this page is built. Every beer below was seen on tap at the venue named, within the last seven days — the same freshness rule the app uses. The page is rebuilt from scratch each night; nothing here is a brewery’s seasonal announcement or a list copied from elsewhere.

A beer earns its place by declaring itself: a name carrying Oktoberfest, Festbier or Wiesn is in. A plain Märzen has to prove it is seasonal rather than a year-round regular, so it is only listed if we have never recorded it outside September and October. That is why Paulaner’s Oktoberfest Märzen appears and Schlenkerla’s smoked Märzen does not.

Missing one? Paste its Untappd link into PINtPOINT and it is in. Background reading: September is Oktoberfest.
