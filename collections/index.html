Curated Collections

Too big to crawl.

A Curated Collection is a themed set of pubs chosen by hand: the surviving tied houses of a brewery that stopped brewing, every pub to have won a national award, the real ones that played somewhere fictional on television. Each is grouped by area, mapped, and built to be ticked off over weeks, months or years.

Not a watchlist, which is yours alone. A collection is a reference you come back to.

Brakspear

The Henley brewer whose name is still over the door of pubs from the Cotswolds to Croydon. A hundred and thirteen of them have a page here, and a Brakspear beer has been logged at a hundred.
