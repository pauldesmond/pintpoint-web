UK Oktoberfest Events 2026

At noon today the new Lord Mayor of Munich, Dominik Krause, needed two blows of the hammer to drive the tap into the first barrel, and shouted “O’zapft is!” Two is a good score. He had said beforehand he hoped for two or three, and it was his first go at it.

The 191st Wiesn runs until 4 October. If watching it has given you ideas, here is where Britain is pouring — sorted by the only question that matters once the oompah starts: is the beer in the stein actually from Munich?

You can watch the whole thing from here. Bavaria’s own broadcaster, BR24, streams the tapping and the rest of the fortnight on YouTube — the parade of the landlords, the Trachten procession on the first Sunday, and the tents themselves. It is in German, and it does not matter in the slightest.

Why that question is worth asking

Only six breweries may pour at the Wiesn — Augustiner, Hacker-Pschorr, Hofbräu, Löwenbräu, Paulaner and Spaten — and what they pour is Festbier: pale, dry, and built to be drunk by the litre for sixteen days. That is the beer being tapped in the tents this afternoon, and it is not the beer the festival started with.

Most British Oktoberfest nights are a German lager and an oompah playlist, which is a perfectly good night out and nobody should be sniffy about it. But a handful put genuine Wiesn beer in the stein, and if you have just watched Krause swing the hammer, you probably want to know which is which. So this list is in two halves, and the first half is short.

Where the actual Wiesn beer is

Go here if you care what is in the glass. One rule for reading this section: “all six” is the venue’s claim, not our count. Both places making it advertise the Big Six without printing a full list, and neither names Augustiner — the one brewery of the six that barely leaves Bavaria. That is an absence from a webpage, not from a bar: it may be poured, or bottled, or neither. Worth asking before you build an evening around it.

19 Sept – 25 Oct

Albert’s Schloss

London (Soho) · Manchester · Birmingham · Liverpool All six claimed

The UK gold standard, and all four cities run the same season — we checked. Free entry, keg tapping, 19 September to 25 October at each. One caveat: their page bills “all of the Munich Big 6”, then names five — Spaten, Paulaner, Löwenbräu, Hacker-Pschorr, Hofbräu. Augustiner is not among them, which would figure: it is the hardest of the six to get out of Munich. It may be there in bottle. Ask.

30 September · 7–10:30pm

Caps and Taps

Tufnell Park, London 3 of 6 — Paulaner, Augustiner, Hofbräu

Which answers the question the paragraph above leaves open. A party keg tapped at seven with hammer and tap, and the £15 ticket is a stein’s worth — two pints — of Paulaner Oktoberfest and a pretzel. Behind it, in bottle: Augustiner, with Weihenstephan, Hofbräu and Flotzinger, plus Sierra Nevada Festbier on keg. This is the only place on this list that names Augustiner — the one of the six that barely leaves Bavaria, and the one both all-six venues decline to print. It took a bottle shop. Sold out, and worth knowing about for next year: they run it every September.

18 Sept – 11 Oct

Kapital

Sheffield All six claimed

The strongest Big Six claim outside Albert’s, and the reason to go north for it: all six Festbiers in 1L steins, with a Paulaner wooden-barrel tapping on Fridays. The site says “all six official Oktoberfest beers” without listing them, and Paulaner is the only one it names.

Kapital

17 Sept – 31 Oct

Munich Cricket Club

Victoria · Tower Hill · Canary Wharf 3 of 6 — Paulaner, Löwenbräu, Spaten

Pouring Paulaner, Löwenbräu and Spaten plus other Munich beers. Multi-week, multi-venue season.

19 Sept – 31 Oct

Bermondsey Bierkeller

London Bridge Paulaner

Long-running cellar with Paulaner on tap and full Bavarian energy. One of the six, done properly as a Bierkeller night out.

Bermondsey Bierkeller

9–24 Oct · nine sessions

Oktoberfest on the Common

Clapham Common Paulaner partner

UK’s big outdoor tent, and by their own account Paulaner’s only official UK Oktoberfest partner — a named Wiesn brewery on the bar at party scale. They pour two south London breweries beside it, Jubel of Lambeth and Sambrook’s of Wandsworth, which no other big tent on this list can say.

Oktoberfest on the Common

21 Sept – 3 Oct

Brigadiers Bier Hall

Bank, London Paulaner

Paulaner steins and towers; Indian–Bavarian food. One of the six in a slightly unexpected room.

Brigadiers Bier Hall

19 September · 6pm

Tiny Rebel Brewery Bar

Rogerstone, Newport 2 of 6 — Hacker-Pschorr, Paulaner

One night, and the most interesting bar on this list for anyone who read the companion essay: Hacker-Pschorr Oktoberfest Märzen at 5.8% alongside Paulaner Oktoberfest Bier — the old amber beer and the modern pale one, on the same bar, on the day Munich tapped. Guest beers, food specials and Oompah Cymru, which is a better band name than anything Bavaria has managed.

@tinyrebelbrewerybar · what’s on tap there now

In the spirit

Same energy, looser about the exact Munich six. Steins, sausages, brass, and a crowd. Dressing up is a bonus rather than the point. Thirty-three of them, by region, so you can find the one near you and stop reading.

Know one we have missed?

This list is forty-one events, and it is certainly not all of them. Seven of them came in from readers within days of publishing — Tiny Rebel, Flat Iron Square, Anspach & Hobday, The Old Brewery, The Dial, The Destination at Lakeside and Caps and Taps — which is exactly how this is supposed to work. Local beer halls, one-night stein parties, a brewery tap doing a Bavarian weekend — if you know one that should be here, tell us and we will add it: hello@pintpoint.co.uk. If you know which beers it actually pours, even better.
