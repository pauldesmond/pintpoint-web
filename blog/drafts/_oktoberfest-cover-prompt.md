# Oktoberfest — cover prompt

Square (1:1), output 1254×1254 JPEG → `/blog/images/oktoberfest-many-festivals.jpg`

Deliberately NOT in the Unholy Trinity's navy night-suite style — this is a
September festival piece, not one of the three autumn nights, and it should
not read as a fourth member of that set.

---

Flat vector illustration in the style of a mid-century screen-printed travel
poster. Limited palette, hard edges, no gradients, no photographic texture,
no 3D rendering. Bold and legible at thumbnail size: one clear focal object,
not a collage of landmarks. Render ONLY the text specified below — do not
invent signage, slogans, captions, brand marks or micro-text anywhere in the
image.

Subject: TWO one-litre Maßkrug beer steins standing side by side on a
scrubbed wooden table, straight on, lit from behind so the beer glows. The
LEFT stein is filled with PALE GOLD beer. The RIGHT stein is filled with DEEP
COPPER-AMBER beer. The colour difference between the two is the entire point
of the image and should be unmistakable at thumbnail size. Both with a white
foam head. Dimpled glass, rendered flat and graphic.

Ground: warm cream or pale straw, like poster stock — NOT navy, NOT night.

Accent palette: pale gold and deep copper for the two beers, white foam,
one muted blue-and-white check as a narrow band or table runner beneath the
steins (Bavarian, but restrained — a stripe, not a tablecloth pattern).

Text on the cover, exactly:
- Top left, small: `PINtPRESS` with `THE PINtPOINT BLOG` beneath it.
- Title, large, above or beside the steins: `Everyone kept a different Oktoberfest`

Avoid: lederhosen, dirndls, pretzels, oompah bands, fairground rides, tents,
Bavarian flags, wheat-beer glasses (wrong vessel — these are Maß steins),
cartoon faces, hands holding the steins, clinking, spilling, autumn leaves,
pumpkins, any "Prost!" or "O'zapft is" lettering.

## Recorded failure modes to watch for (from the trinity covers)
- Generators invent slogans and secondary text. Reject any cover with words
  beyond the two blocks above.
- Generators reach for the deprecated "JOURNALISM FROM PINtPOINT" lockup.
  Current lockup is teal `PINtPRESS · THE PINtPOINT BLOG`.
- If the two beers come out the same colour, the cover has failed its only
  job — regenerate rather than accept.
