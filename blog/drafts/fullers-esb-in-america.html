The beer that came back

Held. His account, attributed as hisAnchor’s beers crossing east in late 1991 in returned Fuller’s kegs, for a trial in three London pubs: the Mawson Arms, the Dove and the Churchill Arms.
  • Held, and the best detail in the section: the kegs were one-way Van Leer barrels that Thames America had bought from Fuller’s. Anchor took the empties, refilled them and shipped them on, and the consequence in his own words is that Anchor ran for years without US Sankey fittings. A shipping decision that outlived the shipment.
