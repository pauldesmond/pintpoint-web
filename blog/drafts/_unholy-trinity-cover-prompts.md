# The Unholy Trinity — cover prompts

Square (1:1). One house style across all three so they read as a suite.

**Shared style block — paste at the top of each prompt:**

> Flat vector illustration in the style of a mid-century screen-printed
> travel poster. Limited palette, hard edges, no gradients, no photographic
> texture, no 3D rendering. Deep navy ground (#0f2a5e-ish) as the dominant
> field, falling snow or night air. Warm lamplight and lit windows as the
> only glow. Bold, legible at thumbnail size: one clear focal object, not a
> collage of landmarks. Render ONLY the text specified below — do not invent
> signage, slogans, captions or micro-text anywhere in the image.

**Text on every cover, exactly:**

> Top left, small: `PINtPRESS` with `THE PINtPOINT BLOG` beneath it.

---

## Part 1 — 31 October — green-man-pub-signs.html

[shared style block]

Subject: a hanging wooden pub sign on a wrought-iron bracket, seen at night
in a quiet street, snowless autumn rain. The sign painting is a FOLIATE HEAD
— a man's face made of oak leaves — painted flat and graphic, like a 1930s
linocut or a signwriter's board. NOT a medieval stone carving, not gothic,
not weathered relic: it should look painted, recent, man-made.

Accent palette: muted sage and moss green for the foliage, parchment cream
for the sign board, one small warm amber from a pub window behind.

Title text, large, left of the sign:
`Season of the Witch`

Avoid: stone texture, churches, gargoyles, anything mystical or glowing,
witches, pumpkins.

---

## Part 2 — 5 November — lewes-bonfire-boy.html

[shared style block]

Subject: a night procession down a narrow English high street. Flat
silhouetted figures in black-and-white horizontally striped jerseys carrying
burning torches and a flaming tar barrel on a pole. Steep tiled roofs and
sash windows close on both sides. Smoke rising into navy.

Accent palette: sulphurous torch-gold and orange for the flames, hard
off-white and black for the striped jerseys, one deep red.

Title text, large, upper left:
`The one that isn't pretending`

Avoid: any effigy, any Pope, any religious figure, guys, fireworks bursts,
crowds of faces. Silhouettes and torches only.

---

## Part 3 — 6 November — christmas-ale-drinking-yule.html

[shared style block]

Subject: a snowy street at night. A dray horse and cart loaded with wooden
barrels at the left, the driver in a BLUE bobble hat and red scarf. On the
right a lit pub window, drinkers in silhouette. Centre, a large hanging
street clock on an iron bracket, the face clean and cream — **hands reading
8:59: the minute hand one minute short of twelve, the hour hand almost at
nine.** The clock is the focal object.

Accent palette: Tuborg-ish blue for the driver's hat, frost white, amber and
gold from the windows, one red.

Title text, large, upper left:
`The First Friday of Christmas`

Avoid: Santa Claus, Christmas trees, landmarks, globes, "around the world"
imagery, any text on the clock face.

## Notes from the first attempts

- The generator put 10:55 on the clock when asked for 20:59. State the hand
  positions in words, and check the render before accepting it.
- It invented signboard slogans ("GOOD BEER TRAVELS FAR", "SAME GOOD CHEER")
  every time until told not to. Hence the no-invented-text instruction.
- It used the deprecated "JOURNALISM FROM PINtPOINT" lockup. Current is
  "PINtPRESS · THE PINtPOINT BLOG".
- Collages of landmarks died at thumbnail size. One focal object each.
