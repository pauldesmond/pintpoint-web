## Why v1 is being replaced

1. **The title.** The card said "The Fuller's yeast that went to California
   and never quite came home." The piece now runs under House's title, and
   Paul wants the card to match the article.
2. **The Golden Gate Bridge and the San Francisco skyline are the wrong
   place.** Firestone Walker is on the Central Coast, roughly 200 miles south.
   San Francisco is Anchor's city, which is the companion piece, not this one.
3. **The steamship.** It reads as the strain being shipped over from London.
   Our piece does not claim that: the yeast was sold as a Fuller's ESB strain
   by Wyeast, a laboratory in Oregon. House corrected us on the crossing once
   already, on the Anchor piece, where the only identified occasion is the
   1983 KQED festival. Not worth leaving a picture of the thing he corrected.

## What replaces the harbour

The Firestone Union, in the brewery's own words on firestonewalker.com:

> "Partially fermented in our patented Firestone Union oak-barrel brewing
> system, DBA quickly took the Central Coast by storm."
> "Our unique double-barrel brewing system was inspired by the original
> 150-year-old 'Burton Union' process."
> "Fermentation and conditioning in individual 60-gallon oak barrels impart
> character unattainable any other way."
> "Since 1996, DBA has been partially fermented in our Firestone Union oak
> barrel brewing system, a nod to the traditional cask-fermented English ales
> that first inspired us."

So the English import in the background is a real one, documented by the
brewery itself, and it is the place this particular yeast actually went to
work. Location also confirmed on their about page: California since 1996,
brewing moved to Paso Robles in 2001.
