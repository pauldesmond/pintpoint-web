    Following the Firkin trail(s)…
    In 1979 David Bruce opened the Goose & Firkin in Elephant and Castle. By 1995 the chain had 44 pubs, 19 of them brewing on site. On 8 October 1999 the brewing stopped — and that, not the 2001 brand-discontinuation, is when Firkin actually died. By 2001 the brand was gone too, but the buildings (mostly still pubs under other names) outlived it. The 12 original London Firkin sites traced — deliberately — the outline of a dog's head on a 1990 paper leaflet. Companion: walk the Firkin Ale Trail Hall-of-Fame crawl in PINtPOINT.
