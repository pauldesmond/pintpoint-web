Augustiner on Draft, Finally

In October 2025 Boak & Bailey reported that James Clay & Sons had apparently convinced the Munich brewery Augustiner to let them distribute its beer on draught in the UK, "with stringent quality control in place", and that James Clay said they would be inspecting glass-cleaning at the pubs serving it. Which pubs those were, nobody said.

James Clay & Sons, the exclusive UK distributor, only officially launched Augustiner Helles on draught in November 2025, and has never published a list of which venues have been accepted onto the programme.

According to Glasgow Live's reporting on Bavaria Brauhaus becoming the first Scottish venue on the programme, seven UK pubs were selling Augustiner Helles on draught in April, though it names none of the other six. We can confirm six pubs from public Untappd check-ins, plus two that came to us directly since: Blind Jack's in Knaresborough, and the Oil Basin Brewhouse in Worcester. James Clay call the draught programme a UK first; Bavaria Brauhaus told Glasgow Live they were serving the beer "once again", so the two accounts do not quite agree.

Eight pouring it, one expected. No news on The Whippet, but we are still hopeful. If you know a pub pouring it that isn't here, let us know. That is how Blind Jack's, confirmed on 11 September, and the Oil Basin, which announced itself on 21 August, both got on the list.

What it takes to pour it

James Clay's published terms make the bar concrete. To be accepted onto the Augustiner draught programme, a venue has to:

  • Run cellar temperature at 4°C
  • Use direct draw from the keg, with no fob chambers
  • Pour at 100% CO₂ top pressure
  • Install 10mm wide-bore lines
  • Take the mechanical line cleaning system James Clay provide, which they call a UK first
  • Meet rate-of-sale expectations and have an existing range that fits

The first two terms are the expensive ones, and they go together. Cask Marque put the industry-standard cellar at 11 to 13°C, which is a cask cellar; a keg lager normally reaches the tap cold because a python chills it on the way. Augustiner's terms take the python out and put the cold in the room, so the whole cellar has to run at lager temperature and the keg has to sit next to the tap. That is pub-cellar surgery for one product. Boak & Bailey write that we have all heard stories of pubs unable to meet the requirements, which is hearsay, but it is the hearsay of two people who would know.

James Clay invites venues to register interest privately. They haven't published the list of accepted operators. What surfaces most of these is the cross-referencing PINtPOINT does by default: each venue's tap list aggregated from its public Untappd check-ins, then normalised by brewery and beer so any one of them can be searched. Blind Jack's came to us the other way round: a reader wrote to say it had started pouring it, and we held the addition until the check-ins caught up with him. The Oil Basin Brewhouse arrived the same way, through a reader, but is here on firmer ground than a check-in: the venue announced it themselves, publicly, in August. An operator saying they have been accepted onto the programme is better evidence than one drinker logging a pint.

Two threads

Mosaic Tap (Newcastle) is on this list and was on another: Pressure Drop's 12 Years of Bosko, which twelve UK venues poured simultaneously on 1 May 2026.

The King's Arms in Bethnal Green is the second pub of the man who runs the Sutton Arms on Great Sutton Street. London Drinker reported that Jack Duignan took its lease over from the GraceLand pub company in November 2024, and that the two pubs ran a joint cask festival the following June. The Sutton Arms was also one of the twelve Bosko venues.

The Whippet angle

The Whippet soft-launched on 14 May 2026 at 76 Old Broad Street, by Liverpool Street station, the first new pub under the Whippet name since the original Holborn Whippet closed in December 2020. Augustiner Helles was one of the lines flagged for opening day, alongside Boddingtons cask served through a sparkler, a Northern signal rarely seen in London.

It isn't pouring Augustiner. The bar's own board, read on 10 September, lists ten keg lines and three casks. Augustiner isn't among them. There is a Bavarian Helles on; it's Tegernseer, at 4.8%.

Our own data agrees but proves less than the board does. Forty-six distinct beers have been logged at the Whippet through public check-ins since it opened, and none has been Augustiner. But check-ins only surface what drinkers happen to log: of the thirteen lines on that board, we were holding five. A beer can sit on a bar for weeks without anyone recording it, which is exactly what happened at Blind Jack's, and the reason a reader knew before we did.

Last time we spoke to them it remained something they intended to do. The Whippet stays on this page as expected rather than confirmed, and it moves up the day it pours.

What we're not claiming

That this is the final UK list. Glasgow Live cited seven pubs on the programme in April. PINtPOINT now confirms eight, the newest being the Oil Basin Brewhouse in Worcester, which announced its own arrival in August and reached us through a reader in October. As more operators make the cellar investment to join the programme, the list will grow. If you know one we've missed, tell us and we'll add it.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I drink Augustiner Helles on draught in the UK?

Eight UK pubs are confirmed pouring Augustiner Helles on draught as of 7 October 2026, following the November 2025 James Clay launch: The King's Arms (Bethnal Green, London), Café Beermoth (Manchester), Society (Barbirolli Square, Manchester), Mosaic Tap (Newcastle upon Tyne), Ball & Chain (Luton), Bavaria Brauhaus (Bothwell Street, Glasgow, the first in Scotland), Blind Jack's (Knaresborough, North Yorkshire), and the Oil Basin Brewhouse (Copenhagen Street, Worcester). The Whippet by Liverpool Street station had Augustiner flagged as an opening line in May but is not pouring it.

When did Augustiner Helles launch on draught in the UK?

Augustiner Helles launched officially on draught in the UK in November 2025, distributed exclusively by James Clay & Sons. The distributor has never published the list of accepted venues, which is why "where to drink it on draught" has been a real question.

Why is Augustiner Helles so hard to find on draught in the UK?

Beyond Augustiner-Bräu München's broader reputation for protecting its product, James Clay & Sons (the UK distributor) requires accepted venues to run cellar temperature at 4°C, use direct keg draw with 100% CO₂ top pressure, fit 10mm wide-bore lines, and commit to mechanical line cleaning. Cask Marque put the industry-standard cellar at 11 to 13°C, so a 4°C cellar with the keg beside the tap means rebuilding the room around one beer.

Is Augustiner Lagerbier Hell the same as Augustiner Helles?

Yes. The brewery's flagship pale lager is officially named Lagerbier Hell. "Helles" is the style designation; "Augustiner Helles" is the colloquial name UK and US drinkers use. Untappd lists it as Lagerbier Hell, which is why our app surfaces it under that name. Same beer.

Did the Whippet pour Augustiner from opening day?

No. Augustiner Helles was flagged as an opening line (14 May 2026) by the venue and the @WhippetEC2 social account. The bar's board on 10 September lists ten keg lines and three casks, and Augustiner is not among them; the German Helles on is Tegernseer. Forty-six distinct beers have been logged there through public check-ins since opening, none of them Augustiner, though check-in data alone would be weak evidence here, since it covered only five of those thirteen lines. Last time we spoke to them it was still something they planned to do.

One thing worth saying in autumn: this is Augustiner Helles, their 5.2% everyday lager. The 6.3% Oktoberfestbier they brew for the Wiesn is a different beer, and it is the one no UK venue on our Oktoberfest list pours on draught. It turns up in bottle instead.
